# Design 13: Disputes to the district, and war (build plan phase 8, group 2)

Status: design, 2026-09-25. Requirements: FR-E E1, E3, E5, E9 (the rest of FR-E, the Commission and politicians, comes with the head rank). Stories §1, contracts §2, content §3, waves §4. New engine allowed; used only for a family-level standing ledger.

## 1. Stories

| Week | The player does | The player sees |
|---|---|---|
| Soldier | Two men of the crew both collect from the same new shop | A card, if one of them is the player: the crew chief calls a sit-down. Approaches: prior claim (who was there first; the claim record decides), an offer (a share of the stall's take to the other man for a year), the chief's favor (spend favor with the chief), or step back. The report says what the chief ruled and why. |
| Soldier, week n | Another family's man collects on the player's stall (the existing poach and hold) | When "hold" fails to settle it, the dispute goes up: the two heads meet before the district head. The player, as the man whose stall it is, gets a card: what to bring to the meeting: the claim record (prior claim), a witness that the other man came armed (evidence, a risk if false), a gift for the district head's family (money, standing), or nothing. Next week the newspaper and the report carry the ruling: the stall stays, goes, or is split; standing between the two families moves; the loser's family remembers. |
| Any | A family refuses the ruling | The district declares the refusing family in the wrong; war follows only if the refusing family's head chooses it (AI: only when Weight ratio favors it and standing with the district is already low). The player sees "Guerra (war)" in the header, the turn contracts to a week, and the Book shows collections halved. |
| War | Four to twelve weeks | Each week: collections at half on both sides, men in hiding do not collect, heat up on both towns, a chance each week of a hit on a man of either family (an operation event, the newspaper reports a body), a meeting offered by the district head every four weeks: accept peace (standing restored slowly, the ruling stands) or refuse (the district turns against the refuser; the Commission's later sanction is a release item). The war ends at peace, or when one family is weakened (design 12) and sues for it. |
| After | The player's family lost a stall to a ruling | The Territory tab shows the district: its families, the district head, and standing bands. The loss is on the map: the stall's sign changes. |

Acceptance: over 200 careers, disputes that reach the district settle without war in at least 80 percent of cases; wars last 4 to 12 weeks with a median near 6; a family at war collects about half; the player's own dispute always produces a card with an approach and a ruling line the next week; standing moves within a turn of a ruling.

## 2. Contracts (owner)

- `world.standing: Record<"famA|famB", SignedMeter>` (owner relationships; symmetric key by sorted ids): family-to-family standing, −1000..1000, fades toward 0 with a 104-week half-life. Fact `StandingDelta { familyA, familyB, delta }`. Predicate fn `relationships.standing { a, b, op, value }` (roles bound to families).
- `District.districtHeadFamilyId` set by generation to the family of highest total Weight in the district; fact `DistrictHeadSet { districtId, familyId }` (owner territory) for later elections.
- `Family.warWith: FamilyId | null` (owner territory), fact `WarStateSet { familyId, enemyFamilyId | null }`. The chains system halves collections for a family at war (design 03 §6) and the exposure system adds heat +5 a week to both families' towns; the family AI keeps chains running. `CharStatus` already has `hiding`: a `StatusChange hiding` man does not collect (chains skip non-free men) and cannot be hit by the war's weekly operation.
- View: `PlayerView.district: { name, headFamilyName, families: Array<{ id, name, yours, state, standing: band | null, atWar: boolean }> } | null`; `PlayerView.crises` already carries `war`.
- Harness: metrics disputes per 100 turns by level (crew, family, district), share settled without war, wars per 100 turns, war length, peace by meeting versus by weakness.

## 3. Content

- `dispute.stall.crew` (spawn when two collectors of one crew claim collisions arise: bind `holder` from a business claim and `rival` as another soldier of the same crew who leaned on or collected from it recently; simplest trigger: a weekly small chance per crew of a stall quarrel between two named soldiers; decision role list `["holder", "rival", "chief"]`): approaches priorClaim, offer, favor, stepBack; outcomes by comparing claim age, favor with the chief and Weight; the loser's loyalty toward the chief drops; a `RecordDelta jobsDone` for a settled quarrel is not given.
- `dispute.district.sitdown`: scheduled by `dispute.claim`'s `heldLost`/`heldWon` when the loser's family refuses (a weighted outcome: 35 percent refuse when their Weight is within 20 percent), roles holderHead, poacherHead, arbiterFamily (the district head), me when the player's stall; decision role list `["holder", "poacher", "arbiterHead"]` where holder or poacher is the player, else the AI heads decide by rules: approaches record, witness, gift, nothing; outcomes: stallStays, stallGoes, split (a `ShareRuleSet`-like effect is not available for a business; use ClaimTransfer or keep, plus a `MemoryAdd` "splitStall" the chain does not read yet: note the gap), each moving `StandingDelta` ±60 and FavorDelta with the district head; the refusing family: `dispute.district.refused` → StandingDelta −150 with the district, then `war.declare` with probability by Weight ratio.
- `war.declare` (exclusiveTag `war` per district): `WarStateSet` both families, `CrisisFlagSet war`, newspaper; `war.week` (spawn each turn while at war, per family): heat +5 both towns, a 15 percent `war.hit` on a random soldier of either family (StatusChange dead or hiding 60/40, newspaper), collections halved by the system; `war.meeting` every 4 turns from the district head: accept (WarStateSet null both, StandingDelta +40 with the district, crisis off) or refuse (StandingDelta −80); `war.exhaustion`: when a family is `weakened`, it sues for peace on the next meeting automatically.
- Newspaper lines for rulings, declarations, hits and peace.

## 4. Waves

Owner: contracts (standing, district head, warWith, chains at war, view district, fn predicate), tests. Wave A: (a) the crew sit-down and the district sit-down with the player's approaches and tests; (b) war templates, hits, meetings, exhaustion, tests, a story `war.story.test.ts`; (c) view and interface (district panel on Territory, war banner in the header, the stall sign), harness metrics and presets (yesMan: hold, record, accept peace; careful: concede, gift, accept; mixed: hold, witness, refuse once). Gate: stories on the starter and three seeds, bands, coverage, catalogue, ten minutes of play.
